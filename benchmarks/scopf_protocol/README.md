# The SCOPF instance protocol (the 41 SCOPF instances of Tables `tab:gpu-scopf` and `tab:scopf-appendix`)

All 41 SCOPF instances of the paper are generated by the protocol below (the manuscript's Algorithm 4, `alg:screen`):
on the seven reference networks, `case118`, `case300`, `case_ACTIVSg200`, `case_ACTIVSg500`, `case1354pegase`,
`case2869pegase` and `case_ACTIVSg2000`, in order A (increasing branch key) at the K values of the paper (23 instances;
"The seven reference networks" below), and on three PGLIB v23.07 networks, `case500_goc`, `case588_sdet` and
`case793_goc`, in the two screening orders A and B at K ∈ {4, 8, 16} (18 instances). This directory holds the scripts
that generated the lists, the records they produced, and two plain-Python builders that rebuild the lists from the
records: `build_lists.py` for the PGLIB networks (output `benchmarks/instances/scopf_pglib_contingencies.jl`, one list of
16 outages per network and order; the instances are `benchmarks/instances/scopf_pglib18.csv`) and `reference_networks.py`
for the reference networks (output `benchmarks/instances/scopf_contingencies.jl`, one list per network; the instances
are `benchmarks/instances/scopf23.csv`).

## The protocol (as stated in the manuscript, Algorithm 4 (`alg:screen`) and the "Instances" paragraph of Section 5)

The manuscript leaves the screening order to this code; the orders A and B are defined in step 3 below.

1. **Base case.** The base AC optimal power flow of the network (`julia/opf.jl`; Ipopt with MA27, at most 500
   iterations; every base solve of the ten networks ended `LOCALLY_SOLVED`, `records/screens/*.meta.txt` and
   `records/reference_networks/screens/*.meta.txt`).
2. **Screen.** Every branch outage is screened as in the reference benchmark: the post-contingency system with the
   base-case controls fixed is solved as **one penalized NCL subproblem** (multipliers 0, **penalty ρ = 1e8**, MadNLP
   with MA57 on the K2r system, tol 1e-8, **at most 200 iterations**), and the outage is **admitted when the scaled
   residual ‖r‖∞ < 1e-4**, whatever the termination status; **bridges are kept** (an outage that islands part of the
   network is screened like any other) (`julia/contingency_pool.jl`, `julia/screen_run2.jl`, `julia/screen_run3.jl`).
3. **Screening orders.** Order **A**: increasing PowerModels branch key. Order **B**: a seeded random order,
   `keys[randperm(MersenneTwister(69), n)]` over the n in-service branches (**seed 69**). Each list of a PGLIB network
   takes the first 16 admitted outages of its screen, in screening order. The reference networks are screened in order
   A only, and each list takes the first n admitted outages, n the largest K of the network's instances (100 for
   `case118`, `case300`, `case_ACTIVSg200` and `case_ACTIVSg500`, 32 for `case1354pegase`, 16 for `case2869pegase` and
   `case_ACTIVSg2000`).
4. **Island rule.** An admitted outage is removed when it islands load that no generator of the island can serve:
   island load > 0 with no unit, or Σ Pmax < load, or Σ Pmin > load (active power only; `julia/bridges.jl`,
   `julia/islands.jl` → `records/islands.csv`, `records/reference_networks/islands.csv`).
5. **Single-contingency verification.** The admitted outages are solved alone, in screening order until the list is full, as the K = 1 SCOPF problem of the
   harness model (`benchmarks/models/scopf_model.jl` with the `gpu.jl` SCOPF settings), by **Ipopt with MA57 at
   tol 1e-8, 10000 iterations, 3600 s** (`julia/k1_verify.jl` with `C3_WALL=3600 C3_MAXITER=10000`). The outage is
   removed when the solve ends `Infeasible_Problem_Detected`, or exhausts its budget with an unscaled constraint
   violation above 1e-3 ("presumed infeasible"); every other outcome keeps it.
6. **Drop and backfill.** Each removed outage is replaced, at the end of the list, by the next admitted outage of the
   same screen (screening order) that passes 4 and 5 (`build_lists.py`, `reference_networks.py`).
7. **Instances.** An instance with K contingencies takes the first K outages of a list: on the PGLIB networks
   **K ∈ {4, 8, 16}**, 3 networks x 2 orders x 3 values of K = 18 instances; on the reference networks the K values of
   the paper, 4 to 100 (23 instances). Each runs with the paper's SCOPF settings (`benchmarks/gpu.jl`: ε = 1e-8,
   τ_relax = 0, objective scaling 1e-4, 3000 iterations, 1800 s).

What the records of the PGLIB networks show (`python3 build_lists.py lists`, `records/lists.md`): of the first 16
admitted outages of the six screens, one, branch **657** of `case588_sdet` order B, was removed by the island rule (island of 2 buses, **52.85 MW**
of load, no unit) and replaced by branch **292**, the next admitted outage of that screen; the 96 list entries all pass
the island rule and all ended `Solve_Succeeded` at K = 1 (657's own K = 1 solve, which finished after the list was
built, ended `Maximum_Iterations_Exceeded` at 10000 iterations with an unscaled violation of 0.40). Budget of the recorded K = 1 solves: the order-B lists of `case588_sdet` and
`case793_goc` ran at the protocol budget (10000 iterations, 3600 s; `records/k1/k1_10000it_3600s/`); the lists of
`case500_goc` (A and B), `case588_sdet` A and `case793_goc` A were verified earlier in the pilot at 3000 iterations and
600 s (`records/k1/c3_3000it_600s/`), and every one of those 64 solves ended `Solve_Succeeded` within that budget (at
most 2380 iterations and 310 s). The longest protocol-budget solve took 3501 iterations (`case793_goc` B, branch 882).

Network eligibility (not part of the tables; records not ported): `case240_pserc` was excluded because the K = 1
verification failed (`Maximum_Iterations_Exceeded`) on 14 of its first 64 admitted outages at 3000 iterations / 600 s
(WO-69 Addendum C3, scopf-pool `benchmarks/pilot/screen/out/c3_k1/`); the 15 wall-capped outages re-solved at
10000 iterations / 3600 s all succeeded (C3b, `benchmarks/pilot/screen/out/c3b_k1/`), and the 14 were not re-run; and the pilot at K = 64 is why the ladder stops at K = 16 (scopf-pool
`benchmarks/results/pilot/`).

## The seven reference networks

The 23 instances of the seven reference networks (`benchmarks/instances/scopf23.csv`) take the first K entries of the
lists of `benchmarks/instances/scopf_contingencies.jl`, which `python3 benchmarks/scopf_protocol/reference_networks.py
lists --check` rebuilds from `records/reference_networks/` (seconds; provenance in its `SOURCE.txt`). The protocol above,
in order A: the screen at penalty 1e8 (`julia/screen_run2.jl`; for `case118`, `case300`, `case_ACTIVSg200` and
`case_ACTIVSg500` the full screen of every in-service branch, order `full` = order A without a stop rule; for
`case1354pegase`, `case2869pegase` and `case_ACTIVSg2000` stopped at 64 admitted), the island rule (`julia/islands.jl` on
the seven networks), the K = 1 verification and drop and backfill. `records/reference_networks/lists.md` (`reference_networks.py
report`) lists every step:

| network | branches screened | bridges (admitted) | admitted | list length n | n-th admission at branch position | dropped / backfilled |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `case118` | 186 | 9 (0) | 156 | 100 | 122 | 0 / 0 |
| `case300` | 411 | 89 (0) | 231 | 100 | 159 | 0 / 0 |
| `case_ACTIVSg200` | 245 | 72 (11) | 179 | 100 | 144 | 0 / 0 |
| `case_ACTIVSg500` | 597 | 254 (33) | 353 | 100 | 157 | 0 / 0 |
| `case1354pegase` | 103 | 33 (7) | 64 | 32 | 52 | 0 / 0 |
| `case2869pegase` | 74 | 4 (2) | 64 | 16 | 16 | 0 / 0 |
| `case_ACTIVSg2000` | 88 | 15 (3) | 64 | 16 | 19 | 0 / 0 |

Neither the island rule nor the verification drops an outage, so each list is the first n admitted outages of its
screen. Seven list entries are bridges (`case_ACTIVSg200` 115, 116, 138; `case_ACTIVSg500` 110; `case1354pegase` 6, 8,
9); each islands one bus without load, and passes the island rule.

Under step 5 (remove an outage only on `Infeasible_Problem_Detected`, or on an exhausted budget with a violation above
1e-3) every one of the 464 entries is kept. The checks at 3000 iterations and 600 s all ended `Solve_Succeeded` and so
pass the protocol budget a fortiori. `case300` was also verified on the MATPOWER 8.1 file, which its K = 100 instance
reads (its K = 10 and K = 50 instances read the 7.0 file): 100 `Solve_Succeeded`, at most 2686 iterations and 125.79 s;
the 100 pairs of rows agree in every column but the label and the time columns, so the two files build the same model.
The records also hold 41 K = 1 rows of admitted outages after the lists (`case1354pegase` 4, `case2869pegase` 24,
`case_ACTIVSg2000` 13), verified ahead of a backfill that was not needed; three of them would be dropped
(`case2869pegase` 18 at a violation of 1.3e-3, `case_ACTIVSg2000` 50 and 53 at 9.1e-3 and 3.1e-3, all wall time).

## Files

| path | contents |
|---|---|
| `julia/opf.jl`, `julia/contingency_pool.jl` | base AC-OPF and the screen subproblem (copies of the reference benchmark's screen, `contingency_problem` unchanged; `screen_candidates` screens a given candidate list) |
| `julia/bridges.jl` | in-service branches and bridges, with the islanded side of each bridge |
| `julia/screen_run2.jl` | the screens of the reference networks (order `full` or A), of the order-A lists of the PGLIB networks and of `case500_goc` B: candidates in order until 16 (B) or 64 (A) are admitted, or every branch (`full`) |
| `julia/screen_run3.jl`, `julia/null_probe_families.jl` | the screen of the order-B lists of `case588_sdet` and `case793_goc`: 300 candidates (run as four slices of 75 with `C2_SLICE=a:b`), also recording the raw residuals |
| `julia/islands.jl` | the island tables (`records/islands.csv`, `records/reference_networks/islands.csv`) |
| `julia/k1_verify.jl` | the K = 1 verification (one row per outage; jobs from `build_lists.py jobs` or `reference_networks.py jobs`) |
| `build_lists.py` | the PGLIB networks: `census` (screening census), `lists` (island rule, verification, drop and backfill; `--write`/`--check` the Julia list file), `jobs` (the K = 1 jobs still missing) |
| `reference_networks.py` | the reference networks: `lists` (the Julia list file `benchmarks/instances/scopf_contingencies.jl`, `--write` / `--check`), `report` (`records/reference_networks/lists.md`), `jobs` (the K = 1 jobs still missing) |
| `records/screens/` | the six screens of the PGLIB networks (`<network>_<order>.csv` + meta files: base status, ρ, candidates, case-file sha256) |
| `records/islands.csv` | the islands of every bridge of the three PGLIB networks |
| `records/k1/<budget>/` | the 97 K = 1 rows of the PGLIB networks (the 96 entries and the replaced outage 657) |
| `records/census.md`, `records/lists.md` | `build_lists.py census` and `build_lists.py lists` on the records |
| `records/reference_networks/` | the reference networks: `screens/` (seven screens + meta files), `islands.csv`, `k1/<budget>/` (the 605 K = 1 rows: the 464 list entries, `case300` on the MATPOWER 8.1 file, and 41 further admitted outages), `lists.md`, `SOURCE.txt` |
| `records/SOURCE.txt` | provenance of the PGLIB records: every record's source path on scopf-pool and its sha256 |

All scripts run on the CPU in `benchmarks/envs/screen` (Julia 1.10.11; MadNCL from this checkout, MadNLP 0.10.1,
PowerModels 0.21.6, JuMP 1.32.1, Ipopt 1.16.0 and the licensed HSL_jll, which must be `Pkg.develop`ed from your own copy:
the Manifest records the path of ours).

## Rebuilding the lists

From the records (seconds; plain Python):

```bash
python3 benchmarks/scopf_protocol/build_lists.py lists --check=benchmarks/instances/scopf_pglib_contingencies.jl
python3 benchmarks/scopf_protocol/build_lists.py lists --write=benchmarks/instances/scopf_pglib_contingencies.jl
python3 benchmarks/scopf_protocol/build_lists.py census
python3 benchmarks/scopf_protocol/reference_networks.py lists --check
python3 benchmarks/scopf_protocol/reference_networks.py lists --write
python3 benchmarks/scopf_protocol/reference_networks.py report --check
```

(`build_lists.py lists --write` also copies the `case118` list of `scopf_contingencies.jl` into the PGLIB file, where
the warm-up solve uses its first entry; write the reference lists first.)

From scratch, the PGLIB networks (CPU; times measured on the campaign machine, 2 x Xeon Gold 6130):

```bash
D=benchmarks/out/scopf_pglib_data; benchmarks/instances/fetch_pglib_scopf_data.sh $D
E="julia --project=benchmarks/envs/screen"; J=benchmarks/scopf_protocol/julia; R=benchmarks/out/scopf_protocol
mkdir -p $R/screens $R/k1/k1_10000it_3600s
# screens (recorded elapsed, meta screen_s: 129 s, 78 s, 176 s, 326 s for 500 A, 500 B, 588 A, 793 A; the order-B
# screens of 588 and 793 ran as four slices of 75 candidates, 181-196 s and 218-228 s per slice)
$E $J/screen_run2.jl $D/pglib/pglib_opf_case500_goc.m  A 64 $R/screens/case500_goc_A  1e8
$E $J/screen_run2.jl $D/pglib/pglib_opf_case500_goc.m  B 16 $R/screens/case500_goc_B  1e8
$E $J/screen_run2.jl $D/pglib/pglib_opf_case588_sdet.m A 64 $R/screens/case588_sdet_A 1e8
$E $J/screen_run2.jl $D/pglib/pglib_opf_case793_goc.m  A 64 $R/screens/case793_goc_A  1e8
$E $J/screen_run3.jl $D/pglib/pglib_opf_case588_sdet.m B 0 300 $R/screens/case588_sdet_B   # 300 candidates, no stop rule
$E $J/screen_run3.jl $D/pglib/pglib_opf_case793_goc.m  B 0 300 $R/screens/case793_goc_B
# islands (9 s)
$E $J/islands.jl $R/islands.csv case500_goc=$D/pglib/pglib_opf_case500_goc.m \
    case588_sdet=$D/pglib/pglib_opf_case588_sdet.m case793_goc=$D/pglib/pglib_opf_case793_goc.m
# K = 1 verification at the protocol budget (recorded: 24-310 s per outage; any number of workers can share one jobs file)
python3 benchmarks/scopf_protocol/build_lists.py --records=$R jobs $D/pglib > $R/jobs.txt
C3_WALL=3600 C3_MAXITER=10000 C3_FIRSTPR=1 $E $J/k1_verify.jl $R/jobs.txt $R/k1_run
cp $R/k1_run/rows/*.csv $R/k1/k1_10000it_3600s/
python3 benchmarks/scopf_protocol/build_lists.py --records=$R lists   # repeat jobs/k1_verify while a check is pending
python3 benchmarks/scopf_protocol/build_lists.py --records=$R lists --check=benchmarks/instances/scopf_pglib_contingencies.jl
```

From scratch, the reference networks (CPU; recorded screen_s 81, 273, 159 and 716 s for the four full screens, 879,
1968 and 1208 s for the three stopped at 64 admitted; K = 1 solves up to 3897 s each):

```bash
D=benchmarks/out/scopf_data; benchmarks/instances/fetch_scopf_data.sh $D
E="julia --project=benchmarks/envs/screen"; J=benchmarks/scopf_protocol/julia; R=benchmarks/out/scopf_protocol/reference_networks
NETS="case118 case300 case_ACTIVSg200 case_ACTIVSg500 case1354pegase case2869pegase case_ACTIVSg2000"
mkdir -p $R/screens $R/k1/k1_10000it_3600s
for n in case118 case300 case_ACTIVSg200 case_ACTIVSg500; do
    $E $J/screen_run2.jl $D/data_mp70/$n.m full Inf $R/screens/${n}_full_rho1e8 1e8
done
for n in case1354pegase case2869pegase case_ACTIVSg2000; do
    $E $J/screen_run2.jl $D/data_mp70/$n.m A 64 $R/screens/${n}_A 1e8
done
$E $J/islands.jl $R/islands.csv $(for n in $NETS; do printf '%s=%s ' $n $D/data_mp70/$n.m; done)   # 45 s
python3 benchmarks/scopf_protocol/reference_networks.py --records=$R jobs $D > $R/jobs.txt
C3_WALL=3600 C3_MAXITER=10000 C3_FIRSTPR=1 $E $J/k1_verify.jl $R/jobs.txt $R/k1_run   # any number of workers per jobs file
cp $R/k1_run/rows/*.csv $R/k1/k1_10000it_3600s/
python3 benchmarks/scopf_protocol/reference_networks.py --records=$R report | sed -n 3,13p   # repeat jobs/k1_verify while a list is pending
python3 benchmarks/scopf_protocol/reference_networks.py --records=$R lists --check
```
